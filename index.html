Welcome

Hi, I'm Nikolaos Dionelis

Research scientist building large-scale, multi-modal AI foundation models for science.

I am a Principal Research Scientist III at the University of Alabama in Huntsville (UAH), working at NASA Marshall Space Flight Center (MSFC) within the Office of Data Science and Informatics (ODSI), on the IMPACT AI for Science team. I develop AI Foundation Models (FMs) for the Sun, Moon, and Earth, in support of NASA missions including the Artemis program and space-weather resilience, aligned with NASA's AI for Science strategy.

Research interests. My research focuses on neural visual tokenization and self-supervised multi-modal learning for scientific imaging: spectral and wavelet-domain tokenizers, finite scalar quantization (FSQ), dual-codebook tokenizers, and multi-modal masked modeling for cross-modal generation. I apply these methods to heliophysics (SDO AIA/HMI), lunar remote sensing (LRO WAC/NAC imagery and digital terrain models), and Earth observation (Sentinel-1/2), with earlier work in generative models, out-of-distribution detection, and statistical signal processing for speech enhancement.

Before UAH/NASA, I was a Research Fellow at the European Space Agency (ESA) Φ-lab at ESRIN, Italy, where I contributed to TerraMind (ICCV 2025), the PhilEO Bench, and the CARE model; a Research Associate at the University of Edinburgh (UDRC); and I hold a PhD and First-Class Honours MEng from Imperial College London.

Current research areas — NASA MSFC ODSI / UAH

Sun · Heliophysics

Solar foundation models

Self-supervised pretraining on SDO AIA and HMI observations, building on the Surya heliophysics foundation model lineage (arXiv:2508.14112), toward space-weather-relevant downstream tasks and long-context solar sequence modeling.

Moon · Lunar remote sensing

Lunar foundation models

Neural visual tokenizers — FSQ, spectral wavelet (DWT/DT-CWT), and dual-codebook designs — for LRO WAC multi-band imagery and digital terrain models, paired with multi-modal masked modeling for cross-modal generation, in support of Artemis-era lunar science.

Earth · EO

Earth observation FMs

Large-scale generative multimodality (TerraMind, ICCV 2025), benchmark design (PhilEO Bench), pretraining scale-up to the MajorTOM dataset, and confidence-aware fine-tuning for segmentation and regression on Sentinel-1/2 data.

Method threads

Visual tokenization for scientific data. Discrete and continuous tokenizers tailored to non-natural imagery: finite scalar quantization hardened for mixed-precision training, wavelet-domain tokenizers that respect the spectral statistics of planetary and solar data, and morphology-aware dual-codebook designs. This work includes matched-compute benchmarking of tokenizer families across reconstruction quality and downstream multi-modal masked-modeling performance.

Evaluation beyond PSNR. Frequency-weighted and physics-aware evaluation metrics for reconstruction and generation — wavelet-subband error weighting, minimal spectral coherence, and local variance error — so that tokenizers and generative models are scored on the structures scientists actually care about.

Confidence-aware learning. Fine-tuning foundation models with calibrated confidence for semantic segmentation (IEEE GRSL 2025) and confidence-aware regression of building density (CARE, IJCNN 2025).

Earlier research

Out-of-distribution detection and generative models (University of Edinburgh, UDRC). GAN-based boundary formation and anomaly detection — TailGAN, BDSG, OMASGAN — few-shot robust classification with OoD detection (FROB, CTR), and similarity learning for X-ray security screening (SLX), within the EPSRC/Dstl University Defence Research Collaboration.

Statistical speech enhancement (Imperial College London). Modulation-domain Kalman filtering for single-channel speech denoising and dereverberation, including phase-aware enhancement, published in IEEE/ACM TASLP and at EUSIPCO and HSCMA.
